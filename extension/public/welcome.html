GPUHUB

Your browser,
put to work.

GPUhub lets AI agents rent your idle GPU and CPU, by the second. You set the price and get paid. Nothing runs until you turn it on.

Get started in three steps

  1. Add your walletClick the GPUhub icon in your toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you can't see it) and paste the address where you want to be paid. A public address is enough. We never ask for a private key.
  2. Set your priceChoose a price in dollars per hour. You can change it whenever you like.
  3. Turn it onUse the switch at the top of the window. By default it only works while you're away from the PC and plugged in.

What a renter can and can't do

Can

  • Run WASM and WebGPU programs in a sandbox
  • Use your GPU and CPU while rented

Cannot

  • Reach your files
  • Reach your network
  • See your other tabs

How you earn

Renters pay by the second, in the GPUhub token. What you've earned shows in the window, and you get a quiet notice when it grows. You can turn those notices off in the window.

When you're ready to collect, open the GPUhub site, go to For PC owners, connect the same wallet and claim.
